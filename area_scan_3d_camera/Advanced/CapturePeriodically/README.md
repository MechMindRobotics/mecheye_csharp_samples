# CapturePeriodically Sample

With this sample, you can warm up a Mech-Eye camera by periodically performing image acquisition over a specified duration to help the device reach stable operating conditions before use.

If you have any questions or have anything to share, feel free to post on the [Mech-Mind Online Community](https://community.mech-mind.com/). The community also contains a [specific category for development with Mech-Eye SDK](https://community.mech-mind.com/c/mech-eye-sdk-development/19).
